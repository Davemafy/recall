# Submission notes

**Project:** RECALL

**Pitch:** RECALL finds the blast radius after a bad legal authority enters a body of legal work.

**Core line:** One hallucination. Find every filing it touched.

**Differentiator:** Citation checkers tell you one citation is wrong. RECALL shows every document that now deserves review because it deterministically cites or reuses that dependency, while keeping semantic similarity in a separate human-review category.

**Technical differentiator:** deterministic reporter/volume/first-page canonicalization and quotation fingerprints stay logically and visually separate from heuristic proposition matching.
